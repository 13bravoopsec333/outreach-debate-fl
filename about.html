— What is Outreach Debate? —

A national movement, rooted in local impact.

Outreach Debate was founded on a simple idea: every student, regardless of zip code or income, should have the chance to experience the transformative power of speech and debate.

What started as a small mentorship program has grown into a national network of chapters across the country — each one building local access through scholarships, coaching, and community.

— Why Debate Matters —

Skills that last a lifetime.

Debate isn't just about winning rounds. It's about learning to think clearly, speak confidently, and engage with the world as an informed citizen.

I

Critical thinking

Research complex topics, evaluate evidence, and construct arguments that hold up under pressure — skills that transfer to every classroom and career.

II

Public speaking

Build the confidence to speak clearly, persuasively, and with composure in front of any audience — from a classroom to a courtroom.

III

Community

Join a network of peers and mentors who share your drive. Debate creates lifelong connections and a sense of belonging.

— What We Do —

Three ways we're building access in Florida.

1

Scholarships

The Orange Blossom Scholarship covers tournament fees, travel, and coaching costs for Florida students who are ready to compete.

2

Mentorship

Free one-on-one mentorship connecting students with experienced debaters who know how to build cases, sharpen delivery, and win rounds.

3

Community

A growing statewide network that gives Florida students a reason to keep going — from first tournament to finals.

OUR STORY FLORIDA
— Our Story —

Built by students, for students.

Outreach Debate Florida launched in 2025 as a student-run initiative to bring the Outreach Debate model to the Sunshine State. We saw Florida students with the talent and drive to compete — but held back by the costs and barriers surrounding debate.

Today, we're building a chapter that combines direct financial support, mentorship, and a statewide community to make debate accessible to every Florida student who wants a seat at the table.
