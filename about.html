Students building access for students.

Outreach Debate Florida launched in 2025 as a student-run chapter of the national Outreach Debate network. We saw Florida students with the talent and drive to compete — held back by the costs surrounding debate.

We raise money, match mentors, and build community so more students can get to their first tournament. That's it. No bureaucracy, no middlemen.

Debate changes everything.

Research topics most adults never touch. Learn to build an argument and defend it under pressure. Stand up and speak clearly in front of a room of strangers. These aren't "soft skills" — they're the foundation of everything that comes after high school.

The Orange Blossom Scholarship

Covers tournament fees, travel, and coaching costs for Florida students who are ready to compete. No essays about your "transformative journey" — just tell us what you need to get to a tournament.

Free one-on-one matching

We pair students with experienced debaters who know how to build cases, sharpen delivery, and win rounds. No group sessions, no waiting. One mentor, one student, one goal.

We started because we had to.

Too many Florida students want to compete in speech and debate but can't afford the entry fees, travel, or coaching. The talent is there. The money isn't. Outreach Debate Florida exists to close that gap — with direct scholarships, free mentorship, and a network that keeps students in the game.

We're a small team. We're student-run. We're building this as we go, and we're not pretending to be bigger than we are. What we do have is a clear mission: more Florida students on the circuit.
