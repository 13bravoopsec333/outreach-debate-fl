— Eligibility —

Who can apply?

The Orange Blossom Scholarship is open to any Florida student who wants to compete in speech and debate — regardless of experience level, school size, or background.

Whether you're a first-time competitor or a seasoned debater heading to state finals, if the financial barriers are holding you back, this scholarship is for you.
