Orange Blossom Scholarship

The scholarship that gets Florida students to tournaments.

What it covers

  • Tournament entry fees
  • Travel and lodging
  • Coaching and research materials

Open to all Florida students. No GPA requirement.

Eligibility

Any Florida student who wants to debate but can't afford the fees. We don't care how many tournaments you've been to or whether your school has a debate team. If cost is in the way, apply.

How to apply

  1. Sign up for updates below
  2. Apply when applications open
  3. Receive your scholarship

We're finalizing the application timeline now. Exact dates will be announced in early 2026 — sign up to hear the moment they're set.
