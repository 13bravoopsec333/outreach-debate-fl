Mentor Program

Free debate mentorship for Florida students.

Apply once. Get matched. Start preparing. No cost, no tryout.

Free · Online · Open to all Florida students

Apply Now How It Works

What mentorship covers

  • Case development — building arguments with better evidence and cleaner organization
  • Speaking drills — practicing clarity, speed, and delivery with real feedback
  • Advanced strategy — prep habits, rebuttal choices, round management

Free · Online · Flexible

Meet on your schedule. No travel required. Mentors are experienced Florida debaters who know the circuits you're competing in.

Any Florida student who wants to get better at debate.

First-timers welcome. State-level competitors welcome. No cost. No tryout. Just fill out the application.

Three steps.

  • 1 Fill out the application — takes about 5 minutes. Share your debate goals, event, and experience.
  • 2 We match you with a mentor — within a week, you'll have a mentor who fits your stage and event.
  • 3 Meet online, sharpen your cases — regular sessions to build stronger arguments and compete with confidence.
