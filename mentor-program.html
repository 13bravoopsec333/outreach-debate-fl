— What You Get —

Better preparation. Braver voices.

The Mentor/Mentee Program connects students across Florida with experienced debate mentors who provide personalized guidance, practice, and feedback. Whether you're preparing for your first tournament or aiming for advanced competition, mentors help you build stronger cases and confidence in every round.

I

Case development

Build arguments with better evidence, cleaner organization, and a strategy you can explain under pressure.

II

Speaking drills

Practice clarity, speed, cross-examination, and delivery with feedback that makes every speech sharper.

III

Advanced debate

Learn the strategic habits behind advanced prep, rebuttal choices, and confident round management.

— Who It's For —

Every Florida student who wants to compete.

Whether you've never been to a tournament or you're prepping for state finals, the program is built for you. We match based on your goals, experience level, and event — so you get support that actually fits where you are.

No cost. No tryout. Just fill out the application and we'll find the right mentor for you.

— How It Works —

Three steps to your first session.

1

Apply

Share your debate goals, event, and experience in a short application. Takes about 5 minutes.

2

Match

We pair you with an experienced mentor who fits your next stage of growth.

3

Grow

Meet online, sharpen your cases, and build confidence round after round.
