Label: **WHAT MENTORSHIP LOOKS LIKE**

The Mentor/Mentee Program connects students across Florida with experienced debate mentors who provide personalized guidance, practice, and feedback. Whether you're preparing for your first tournament or aiming for advanced competition, mentors help you build stronger cases and confidence in every round.

## Section: Three ways to grow

Label: **THREE WAYS TO GROW**

## Practice the work that changes your round.

### Case development

Build arguments with better evidence, cleaner organization, and a strategy you can explain under pressure.

### Speaking drills

Practice clarity, speed, cross-examination, and delivery with feedback that makes every speech sharper.

### Advanced debate

Learn the strategic habits behind advanced prep, rebuttal choices, and confident round management.
