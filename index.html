The Orange Blossom Scholarship

Applications open 2026

Tournament fees, travel, coaching, and research resources shouldn't determine who gets to compete. This scholarship supports Florida students who are ready to speak up, learn deeply, and belong.

— Access —

Access changes the conversation.

Our chapter is creating more on-ramps: direct scholarships, local mentorship, and a statewide network that gives students a reason to keep going.

I

Direct financial support

Tournament fees, travel, and coaching costs covered for students who are ready to compete but held back by the numbers.

II

Local mentorship

Free one-on-one mentorship connecting students across Florida with experienced debaters who know how to win.

III

A statewide network

A growing community that gives Florida students a reason to keep going — from first round to finals.

— Mentor Program —

Three ways to grow.

Practice the work that changes your round — with a mentor in your corner.

I

Case development

Build arguments with better evidence, cleaner organization, and a strategy you can explain under pressure.

II

Speaking drills

Practice clarity, speed, cross-examination, and delivery with feedback that makes every speech sharper.

III

Advanced debate

Learn the strategic habits behind advanced prep, rebuttal choices, and confident round management.
