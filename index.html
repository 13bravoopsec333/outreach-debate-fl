The Orange Blossom Scholarship

Access changes the conversation.

Debate builds critical thinking, research fluency, and the confidence to be heard. Yet too many Florida students are sidelined by the costs surrounding competition.

01 — Scholarships

Direct financial support

Tournament fees, travel, and coaching costs covered for students who are ready to compete but held back by the numbers.
