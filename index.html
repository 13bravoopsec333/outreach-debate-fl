What we do

Outreach Debate Florida is a student-run nonprofit that funds tournament fees, travel, and coaching for Florida students. We pair students with experienced mentors and build a statewide network so nobody figures this out alone.

Orange Blossom Scholarship

Applications open 2026

Tournament fees, travel, coaching, and research resources — covered. No essays about why you deserve it. Just tell us what you need.

What it covers

  • Tournament entry fees
  • Travel and lodging
  • Coaching and research materials

Open to all Florida students. No GPA requirement.
